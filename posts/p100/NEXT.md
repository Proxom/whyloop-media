# p100 lotion / soap pump "HOW DOES IT PULL / THE SOAP UP?" - track A (Kokoro af_heart) - group E - v1 (20 Nov 01:10 run, fork) - planned slot 26 Nov 2026 21:00 (coordinator schedules)
topic key: lotion pump (synonyms: soap pump, dispenser pump) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked 89 rows; schrader valve p085 / bike pump p078 are other objects).
Principle: a piston in a chamber + a return spring + two one-way ball valves. Press: chamber pressure shuts the bottom (inlet) ball and lifts the top (outlet) ball -> soap goes up the stem and out. Release: the spring lifts the piston -> suction shuts the top ball and lifts the bottom ball -> soap is drawn up the dip tube into the chamber.
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop, lightRig, spring, glow, TXT, S helpers) + scene-local lathes (FULL + back half with hatched red section caps on z = 0): clear PET bottle (pale-blue wall section) + green soap, wooden counter (S.woodTex), white ribbed collar, PP chamber with seat cone + spring ledge, dip tube, rubber piston, outlet-ball pocket, hollow stem, white head + nozzle; chrome balls, steel spring, finger (capsule + nail), instanced soap stream; soap inside the pump as translucent fills; 2D flow dots along the real paths, rings on the balls (red = shut, green = open).
