topic key: thermostatic radiator valve (synonyms: trv, radiator thermostat head, wax thermostat) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (checked all rows + mechanism: kettle switch p075 = bimetal strip snap; toilet fill valve p072 = float lever; here the ONE idea = wax capsule expands with room heat, pushes the pin that shuts the hot-water valve; spring reopens it on cooling).
Principle: a TRV head holds a wax (or liquid/gas) capsule that expands as the room warms and pushes the valve pin, pressing the disc toward the seat until the hot-water flow stops; when the room cools the capsule contracts and the valve's return spring pushes the pin back, reopening the valve.
