topic key: flip key (synonyms: folding car key, switchblade key, flip key fob) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked). One idea: a wound torsion spring + a button whose teeth lock it in notches.
Principle (US 7,370,501 + US 8,978,429): a torsion spring around the pivot urges the blade from folded to open; the coaxial push button (held up by a small coil spring) has two legs that sit in the swivel AND in notches of the case -> locked; pressing drops the legs out of the case notches -> the spring snaps the blade out until it rests on the case nose (stop); the button spring pushes the legs back into the notches (2 notches 180 deg apart serve both positions) -> locked open. Folding back winds the spring again.
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop + light pool, contactBlob, lightRig, spring x2, glow, TXT) + scene-local: soft-touch fob body (RoundedBox), rubber buttons, chrome seam + key ring, pivot head as LatheGeometry shells (full outside; back half + red ShapeGeometry section caps + grey notch walls in the cutaway), slotted swivel ring, extruded polished blade with groove inset, button with two brass legs.
