topic key: pepper grinder (synonyms: pepper mill, conical burr grinder, salt and pepper mill) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (checked all rows + mechanism: pencil sharpener p081 = cone + blade shaving; here = rotating grooved cone in a fixed grooved ring, tapered gap).
Principle: a grooved cone turns inside a fixed grooved ring; the gap narrows downward, so peppercorns are cracked smaller and smaller until they fit through the bottom gap; tightening the top knob pulls the shaft + cone further into the ring -> smaller gap -> finer grind.
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop, lightRig, TXT, S helpers) + scene-local: walnut lathe body + turning head (half-cut lathe with wood end-grain caps), dark steel ring with outward grooves (parametric) and red section caps, bright steel cone with helical inward grooves (parametric), shaft, chrome knurled knob, 24 peppercorns in the chamber (kin.py, no overlaps), 10 flying corns x 4 pieces in the gap, 140 exit bits sized from the bottom gap.
