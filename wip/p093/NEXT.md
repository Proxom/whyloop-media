Principle: a TRV head holds a wax (or liquid/gas) capsule that expands as the room warms and pushes the valve pin, pressing the disc toward the seat until the hot-water flow stops; when the room cools the capsule contracts and the valve's return spring pushes the pin back, reopening the valve.
