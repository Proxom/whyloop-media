topic key: pressure cooker weight (synonyms: jiggler, pressure regulator weight, weighted valve) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked 95 rows).
Principle: the weight plugs the vent pipe; steam pressure x hole area pushes it up; when the push beats the weight it lifts, steam escapes, the pressure drops a little and it falls back -> the weight sets the top pressure (about 15 psi gauge on a standard US cooker: Presto, HyperPhysics, Wikipedia).
