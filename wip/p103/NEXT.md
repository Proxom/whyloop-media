# p103 prismatic day/night rear-view mirror "WHY DOES THE TAB / DIM THE LIGHTS?" - track A (Kokoro af_heart) - group V - v1 (20 Nov 20:10 run, fork) - planned slot 27 Nov 2026 01:00 (coordinator schedules)
topic key: rearview mirror dimming (synonyms: prismatic day/night mirror, rear-view mirror flip tab) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked 95 lines; no mirror/optics row).
Principle: the glass is a wedge (thicker at the top) with a silvered back; light from behind reflects strongly off the silver and weakly (~4 %) off the plain glass front, at different angles because the surfaces are not parallel; the tab tilts the whole mirror so the bright silver reflection misses the eye and only the 4 % front reflection reaches it (headlights dim but visible).
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop, lightRig, RBox, glow, TXT) + scene-local parts: wedge glass (extruded cross-section, exaggerated 8.1 deg), silver back layer, black housing in two halves (near half hides = cutaway), bezel lips, tab on a hinge, ball stem to the roof/windshield corner, roof liner + windshield with frit band, mirror face plane with a canvas "rear window + headlights" view whose brightness drops in night mode + 2D glare blobs.
kin.py (1/120 s) checks PASS: day pose solved so the silver back reflects the car behind into the eye (180.3 vs 180.3 deg), night = day - wedge angle (glass front shows the car, silver looks at the roof), housing never touches the roof (min 46 mm) or crosses the windshield, tab always below the housing, mirror tilts only while the tab moves (same easing).
Rays (overlay) are computed live from the current tilt: incoming beam -> glass front F (faint 4 % bounce) -> silver back B (bright bounce), straight lines (refraction ignored, said in the first comment).
Story: hook 0-2.3 s front view, glare blazes, tab flips at 0.55-0.9 s, glare drops + question + red circle on the tab; 1 THE GLASS IS A WEDGE (side close-up, THICK TOP / THIN BOTTOM / SILVER BACK / PLAIN GLASS); 2 DAY: THE SILVER SHINES IN (wide side diagram, car behind + eye icons, bright beam to the eye, 4 % beam goes low); 3 FLIP THE TAB: IT ALL TILTS (close-up on the tab flip, then wide: BRIGHT: MISSES YOUR EYE); 4 NIGHT: ONLY THE GLASS (ONLY 4% REACHES YOU, then front view DIM, BUT STILL VISIBLE); answer card 21.4-25.6 s + follow line; loop = flash cut to the hook.
