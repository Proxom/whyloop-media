# p105 bicycle bottle dynamo "HOW DOES THE WHEEL / LIGHT THE LAMP?" - track A (Kokoro af_heart) - group V - v1 (20 Nov 20:10 run, fork) - planned slot 27 Nov 2026 01:00 (coordinator schedules; replaces p103 rear-view mirror: visual 4 + clarity NO)
topic key: bike dynamo (synonyms: bottle dynamo, sidewall dynamo, bicycle generator) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked 95 rows; speedometer p092 = magnet drags a cup, different object/idea).
Principle: the tire sidewall spins a small ribbed roller (no slip: roller speed = tire speed at the contact / roller radius); the roller turns a permanent magnet inside a fixed copper coil; the changing field induces a current (Faraday) that runs to the lamp; faster = more, stopped = none.
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop, lightRig, glow, TXT, RBox) + scene-local parts: 700c-style wheel (torus tire 320/18 with a tan gumwall canvas texture, alu rim lathe, 32 wire spokes, hub), green enamel fork + crown + steerer, dynamo (lathe bottle body + coil with a bumpy wire profile, rebuilt per frame for the 0-160 deg cutaway with hatched red section caps), ribbed roller with one yellow marker rib, red/blue 2-pole magnet, clamp + bracket to the fork blade, black headlight (lathe) with chrome bezel, emissive lens, additive beam cone and a point light, wire as a CatmullRom tube; yellow electricity dots drawn along the curve (speed and count follow the wheel speed).
Lessons from p103 applied: object fills the frame in every shot (hook: wheel + dynamo + lamp; step 1 macro on the roller; step 2 dynamo cut open fills the frame; steps 3-4 dynamo + wire + lamp), camera still during each key motion (moves only on the cuts), no abstract diagrams.
kin.py (1/120 s) checks PASS: roller touches the tire sidewall with no penetration over its length; dynamo body clear of the tire; body vs fork blade min 70 mm; magnet inside the coil with clearance; roller/magnet angle = wheel angle x 26.67 exactly; lamp brightness 0 exactly when the wheel is stopped.
