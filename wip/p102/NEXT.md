# p102 pencil tire pressure gauge "HOW DOES THE STICK / KNOW THE PRESSURE?" - track A (Kokoro af_heart) - group V - v1 (20 Nov 01:10 run, fork) - planned slot 26 Nov 2026 17:00 (coordinator schedules; replaces p101 clipless pedal, visual 4)
topic key: tire pressure gauge (synonyms: pencil gauge, stick gauge) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked; schrader valve p085 = the valve, a different object).
Principle: pressing the chuck on the valve pushes the valve pin; tire air pushes a sealed rubber piston along the tube against a calibrated spring until they balance (travel ~ pressure); the piston pushes a loose numbered stick out; when the gauge is lifted the air escapes and the spring returns the piston, but friction keeps the stick out so you can read it.
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop, lightRig, spring, glow, TXT) + scene-local lathes: chrome chuck + tube + stop cap (full for outside, back half + hatched red section caps for the cutaway), knurl rings, pocket clip, rubber piston, steel spring (setLength), blue stick cap + white stick with a canvas scale (marks every 2 psi, numbers every 10, 1 mm per psi), brass valve stem + core pin on a flat-lying wheel (alloy rim + tire torus + tread blocks). Valve + gauge axis tilted 28 deg (diagonal fills the portrait frame).
kin.py (1/120 s) checks PASS: stick never inside the piston, piston never passes the stick, stick cap never hits the stop, cap inside the spring, spring inside the tube, rod through the stop hole, valve stem never through the seal, valve open exactly when the gauge pin pushes the core pin >= 1.5 mm, piston rises only while the valve is open, stick moves only when the piston pushes it.
Story: hook 0-2.3 s gauge on the valve, stick shoots out to 32 (payoff) + question + red circle on the stick exit; cut inside: 1 PRESS IT ON: AIR RUSHES IN (pin opens the valve, blue air dots in); 2 AIR PUSHES THE PISTON UP (slow, stick pushed out); 3 A SPRING PUSHES BACK (up/down arrows, BALANCED HERE); 4 LIFT IT OFF: THE STICK STAYS (piston back, FRICTION HOLDS IT); 5 READ THE NUMBER (close-up, 32 psi at the tube end); answer card + follow line; loop = flash cut to the hook.
