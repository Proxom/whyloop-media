topic key: clipless pedal (synonyms: SPD pedal, cleat binding, clip-in pedal) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked; bike freewheel / v-brake / schrader are different objects).
Principle (Shimano UM-45F0A + UM-L800B + Shimano SPD page + US 6,341,540): the cleat goes in toe first under the front jaw (forward + downward motion); pushing the heel down, the cleat's end cams the spring-loaded back jaw open until it snaps over the cleat; a single-release cleat cannot be pulled up out (only a heel-out twist releases it); the twist turns the cleat so its corner pushes the back jaw open; a bolt at the rear of each binding sets the spring force.
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop + pool, lightRig, spring x2, glow, TXT) + scene-local: brushed alu pedal body (extruded side profile), steel front jaw (fixed) and back jaw on a pin (extruded side profiles = the kin.py polygons), spring stop post, axle + black crank arm, gold cleat (plan shape with a rounded front, R 15) + black boss, rubber sole + smooth shoe upper (spline profile, bevelled) + white stripe; sole/upper go transparent (0.28) inside, upper hidden in the twist view.
kin.py (120 Hz): PASS - side view (shapely): cleat never overlaps the front jaw (min gap 0.23 mm) or the back jaw (back jaw angle = smallest non-overlap angle by bisection, closes at <= 260 deg/s like a spring snap), boss clears both jaws, cleat never below the pedal top; the back jaw only opens when the cleat needs room (no opening without a cause); locked during the pull-up (lift = 0.15 mm clearance only); plan view twist: jaw pushed back by d(theta) = 20.6 cos + 15 sin - 21.1 (corner about the pivot), release when d >= 2.5 mm lip overhang (theta 13.7 deg), lift only after release; loop ends match.
Timeline: hook 0-2.2 s (real-speed click-in at ~0.7 s + question + red circle on the binding); 1 cleat hooks under the front jaw (side view); 2 heel down, cleat pushes the back jaw (slow, springs glow); 3 snap at T.snap (10.2 s) CLICK: LOCKED; 4 pull up: still locked (HELD HERE / AND HERE); 5 twist view from above: HEEL OUT arc, CORNER PUSHES THE JAW, FREE; answer card + follow line, back to the hook pose (loop).
