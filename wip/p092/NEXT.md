topic key: speedometer (synonyms: eddy current speedometer, speed cup, analog speedometer) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (checked all rows + mechanism: spring scale p068 = spring vs weight; here the ONE idea is the spinning magnet dragging the non-magnetic cup with NO contact; hairspring only as the counterforce).
Principle: a magnet spun by the speedometer cable induces eddy currents in the aluminum speed cup around it; the drag torque (proportional to spin speed) turns the cup + needle against a hairspring until the torques balance, so the needle angle grows with speed, with an air gap and no contact.
