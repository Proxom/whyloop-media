Principle: a self-parking wiper motor has a park switch on its final gear (sliding contacts on a copper plate, or a cam-pushed switch); with the dash switch off the motor is still fed through this switch until the gear reaches the park position, where the contact opens (and the armature is usually shorted to brake it), so the blades stop at the bottom.
