# p094 prismatic day/night rear-view mirror "ONE FLICK, / NO GLARE?" - track A (Kokoro af_heart) - group V - v1 (HARD GATE PASS; clarity + visual reviews pending, coordinator) - target slot 21 Nov 2026 21:00 (replaces p090 wiper, visual 4)
topic key: rearview mirror day night (synonyms: prismatic mirror, day/night mirror tab, anti-glare mirror) - not in learnings/topics-used.md (coordinator checked the registry).
Principle: the glass is a wedge (thicker at the top) silvered on the back, so the headlights give two reflections at different angles: ~90 % from the silver back and ~4 % from the uncoated front. Day: the bright back reflection is aimed at the driver's eyes. Flipping the tab tilts the whole mirror up: the bright reflection goes up to the roof and only the faint 4 % front reflection reaches the eyes (dim, still visible).
Built from kit/kit.js (makeKit, materials, backdrop, lightRig, RBox, glow, TXT) + scene-local: housing / glass wedge / silver layer as side-profile extrusions in two halves (the near half fades for the section; grey housing section, cyan glass section, bright silver line), rounded end caps, tab lever, neck + chrome ball + stem, fabric roof liner, a printed 'reflected rear view' plane (day bright / night dim) inside the glass for the front shots.
kin.py (2D ray optics, Snell n 1.52, side plane): tilt solved by bisection so the back reflection hits the eye (day, tilt -13.9 deg) / the front reflection hits the eye (night, +6.9 deg). Checks PASS: day front ray misses the eye by 42.8 deg; night back ray misses by 45.4 deg and lands on the roof (s 176, y 95); hit points stay 15-85 % inside the glass faces; tab never enters the housing; tilt only changes while the tab moves (one driver u).
