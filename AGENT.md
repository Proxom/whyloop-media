## VISUAL QUALITY FIX — CONCRETE (genel müdür, 6 Nov 2026 18:35, after Erkan said the 17:00 door Reel again looked low quality)

Rules for every Reel (part of QC):
1. RENDER SETTINGS (three.js): renderer.toneMapping = ACESFilmic, outputColorSpace sRGB, physically correct lights; an environment map for reflections (PMREM from RoomEnvironment or a free CC0 HDRI from Poly Haven); MeshPhysicalMaterial/MeshStandardMaterial with real roughness/metalness and CC0 PBR textures (wood, metal, paint, plastic) incl. normal and roughness maps; soft shadows (PCFSoftShadowMap or VSM, 2048+ shadow maps) on every object and the floor; ambient occlusion (SAO/N8AO pass); subtle bloom on highlights; a gentle vignette.

4. MOTION BLUR AND SMOOTHNESS: render 60 fps; for fast motion accumulate 4-8 sub-frames per output frame (temporal supersampling) for real motion blur; render at 1.5-2x resolution and downscale for anti-aliasing.
5. ENCODE: H.264 High, CRF 17-18, about 12-20 Mbps, yuv420p, 1080x1920; audio AAC 256 kbps.
6. PHYSICAL CORRECTNESS: nothing passes through anything (doors stay inside their frames, hinges at the right edge, parts touch where they should).

8. ## NO HARSH HIGH-PITCHED SOUNDS — HARD RULE (Erkan, 5 Nov 2026 18:57 — overrides every older audio rule in this file)
Erkan: there must be no disturbing high, shrill (tiz) sounds in any video. This is part of the zero-defect quality gate; a video that breaks it fails QC.
1. BANNED sounds: beeps, sine or square tones above about 1.5 kHz, whistles, chimes, bells, pings, "ding" UI sounds, glass/metal clinks with a sharp attack, high synth leads, hiss, squeaks, sirens, sparkle/shimmer SFX, risers that climb into a whine, and any repeated high click or tick. Marble/plank "note" sounds and counters/timers must use soft, low or mid tones (wood, felt, soft marimba below about 1 kHz fundamental), never bright bells.
2. MIX: put a gentle low-pass on all music and sound effects (cut above about 7-8 kHz, 12 dB/oct) and a high-shelf of about -4 dB from 5 kHz on the final mix. Voiceover may keep its natural brightness but use a de-esser (e.g. ffmpeg `deesser`) so S sounds are soft. Keep the loudness rules (about -20 LUFS for music-only videos, voice videos as stated elsewhere) and true peak at or below -2 dBTP.
3. MEASURE before scheduling (add to QC, write the numbers in the dashboard doc): A) whole mix integrated loudness; B) the same audio through `highpass=f=4000` measured the same way. B must be at least 18 dB below A. Also scan with `astats` or a spectrogram (`showspectrumpic`) for any narrow, sustained line above 2 kHz (a tone). If B is too close to A, or any tone line appears, fix the audio and measure again. Finally LISTEN check: describe in the QC note what the high frequencies contain; if anything could sound shrill on phone speakers, fix it.
4. EXISTING VIDEOS (Erkan, 5 Nov 20:03): videos that are already produced and scheduled are NOT re-tested for high-pitched sound; leave them as they are. This rule applies only to NEW videos produced from now on.

## AUDIO QUALITY UPGRADE — NO MORE CHEAP SYNTH SOUND (Erkan, 30 Oct 21:27: "the audio of the videos so far is not high quality and a bit annoying; we must improve it")
- Banned: raw sine/square/saw beeps, white-noise bursts used as impacts, constant hiss, harsh high clicks, loud sub-bass thumps, mechanical-sounding repeated identical samples, anything shrill.
- Build sound like a real sound designer (all in code, free):
  1. Impacts with MODAL SYNTHESIS: each hit = a sum of 4-8 decaying sine partials at inharmonic ratios typical of the material (wood block ~ short, low partials, 60-150 ms decay; metal bar/bell ~ longer shimmering partials, 300-1500 ms; glass/marble ~ bright but short; xylophone/marimba bars with tuned fundamentals). Add a very short soft noise "contact" transient (1-5 ms, band-limited).   2. Melodic content on a musical scale (pentatonic or major), notes in the comfortable 300-2000 Hz range, never piercing.
  3. SPACE: add a gentle synthetic reverb (convolution with a generated exponentially decaying stereo noise impulse of 0.6-1.2 s, wet about 15-25 %), and stereo panning from the object's screen position (equal-power pan). Keep it natural, not washy.

## QUALITY BAR v2 (Erkan, 30 Oct: "much more realistic, much more impressive" — this overrides any earlier "good enough")
Every video must look like a premium studio production, not a code demo. Before publishing, watch frames at 0s, 1s, mid and end (extract PNGs and LOOK at them) and score the video 1-10 on: hook (stops the scroll in 1 s), visual polish, realism, motion smoothness, sound, payoff. Publish only 8+; otherwise rebuild. The independent reviewer subagent must judge against this list, not just for errors.
- Realism of rendering: use three.js (WebGL via Playwright/chromium, `--use-gl=swiftshader` or angle) or high-quality canvas with real lighting: PBR/metal and glass materials, soft shadows, ambient occlusion look, depth of field, bloom, film grain/vignette, subtle camera moves (dolly, orbit, handheld sway), reflections. No flat 2D clip-art look, no pure blueprint-line look unless it is a deliberate style with glow, depth and motion.
- Smoothness: render at 30 fps minimum (60 fps source when cheap), ease every motion (never linear), add motion blur by sub-frame accumulation (4-8 sub-samples per frame), overlap actions, anticipation and follow-through. Encode high quality (libx264 CRF 16-18, preset slow when time allows, yuv420p, 1080x1920 FULL FRAME, faststart).
